Our school

A school built around what children can become.

At Bruno, children are respected as capable learners who thrive when given meaningful work, caring guidance, and room to explore.

Who we are

Big possibilities begin with small discoveries.

Bruno Montessori Academy is a Montessori school in Birmingham, Alabama, with programs from toddlerhood through middle school.
Our approach supports children's academic, social, emotional, and physical development through meaningful, hands-on learning.

Bruno's published mission emphasizes independent lifelong learners and a community celebrating respect, diversity, and inclusion.
