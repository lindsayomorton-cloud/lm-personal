Learning at every stage

A place to grow, year after year.

From the first steps toward independence to the confidence of middle school.

15 months–2 years

Toddler

The Bruno toddler program provides a holistic approach to early childhood education, focusing on the whole child's development – socially, emotionally, cognitively, and physically – in a supportive and enriching environment. Teachers observe each child closely to understand their unique interests, strengths, and areas for growth. Activities and lessons are tailored to meet each toddler's needs, allowing for personalized learning experiences.

Ages 3–5

Primary

Our Montessori primary classrooms provide a vibrant and inclusive learning environment where diversity is celebrated, and every child feels welcomed and engaged. Our carefully designed classrooms are tailored to facilitate joyful learning experiences that cater to each student's individual social, emotional, and academic development of each student.

Grades 4–6

Upper Elementary

Our Upper Elementary program offers a dynamic and modern educational experience within a newly renovated environment. We prioritize the holistic development of our students. They actively participate in their education, cultivating enthusiasm and a sense of ownership. We spark curiosity and nurture critical thinking habits by engaging in STEM and cultural activities, presented in both whole and small group lessons.

Grades 7–8

Middle School

Discover excellence in Bruno’s middle school program, where we nurture a holistic approach to education. Our focus extends beyond academics to encompass community involvement, emphasizing the development of well-rounded individuals. Rigor is woven into our curriculum, challenging students to excel academically while fostering self-reflection and self-actualization.
