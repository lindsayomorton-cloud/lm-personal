Toddler15 months–2 years

The first “I can do it.”

The Bruno toddler program provides a holistic approach to early childhood education, focusing on the whole child's development – socially, emotionally, cognitively, and physically – in a supportive and enriching environment.
Teachers observe each child closely to understand their unique interests, strengths, and areas for growth.
Activities and lessons are tailored to meet each toddler's needs, allowing for personalized learning experiences.
