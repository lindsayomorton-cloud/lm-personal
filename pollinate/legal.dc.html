01 · Disclosures

How offerings work here.

Members’ circle

Opportunities in the members’ circle are private offerings open only to approved members who qualify as accredited investors. Fern Gully shares research to support each member’s own decision. Members decide independently whether to invest.

Community rounds

Community rounds are offered under Regulation Crowdfunding through a registered funding portal and FINRA member, named on each round page. Fern Gully is not the intermediary, does not hold investor funds, and does not give investment advice on community rounds.

Risk

Investing in early-stage companies is risky. You could lose your entire investment. Securities bought in community rounds generally cannot be resold for one year, and there may never be a market for them. Read each company’s Form C before investing.

Conflicts

Fern Gully and its partners may invest in, advise or receive fees from companies shown on this site. Each opportunity page will disclose these relationships.

02 · Privacy

What we collect, and why.

Information you give us

Applications, project submissions, investment details and messages you post. We use them to review applications, run the community and communicate with you.

Sharing

Project submissions are shared with our research team and, if a project moves forward, with members. Investment details for community rounds are shared with the funding portal. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by writing to privacy@ferngully.example.

03 · Terms

Using this site.

Accounts

Keep your sign-in private and your information accurate. We may suspend accounts that misuse the site.

Content

Questions and comments you post may be visible to other members or the public. Be respectful and do not share confidential information you aren’t allowed to share.

No advice

Nothing on this site is investment, tax or legal advice. Talk to your own advisers.
