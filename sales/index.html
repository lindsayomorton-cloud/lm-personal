// why me

Four reasons I'd be your strongest rep

01

I've sat in your buyer's chair

I've led marketing at company after company, which means I've been the person martech sellers were trying to reach. I know who signs off, which objections are real, and how to tie a product to the pipeline number a marketing leader is measured on.

02

I build my own pipeline

I don't wait for leads. I've spent 15 years creating demand with content, social and ABM, and I'll use the same tools to source my own deals. You won't have to wait on marketing to feed my number.

03

I wrote the playbook reps run

Everywhere I've led marketing, I've worked right alongside sales, from building go-to-market motions from scratch to launching AI products on tight timelines. I know how to build a motion and how to sell something that didn't exist last quarter.

04

I did the discovery before the discovery call

As a marketing lead, my research is why buyers got on the call in the first place: who they are, what they care about and what's keeping them up at night. So I don't start from zero. On the call, I'm fine-tuning: learning their language, how their company actually works and who really makes the decision. My journalism training helps too. I ask the question behind the question, then actually listen.
