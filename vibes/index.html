OUR STORY

Vibes Candy was born in the sun-soaked soul of Venice Beach, California — infused with the unique flavor of its vibrant beach community, where sand and surf meet street art and skate culture.

We created Vibes to fulfill our own cravings for a wholesome candy that tastes amazing, and is actually good for you.

Our ethos lies in authenticity: real ingredients, real health benefits, and real connection. We bring fresh dimensional flavors to every moment. Catch your vibe!
